# sendcutsend changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Upload parts from inline DXF text or through a pre-signed upload URL, and wait for SendCutSend to process them.
- Price a part in every material, cheapest first, or in one material with price breaks and delivery dates.
- Price and order bending, tapping, powder coating, anodizing, plating, deburring and tumbling, checked against the part's geometry and SendCutSend's published rules first.
- List a part's bend lines and holes, the taps that fit each hole, and its saved services.
- Build saved carts and return a checkout URL the account owner opens to pay. Nothing is ordered here.
- List and delete parts and saved carts, and list materials with their services.
