# sendcutsend changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Upload parts from inline DXF text or through a pre-signed upload URL, and wait for SendCutSend to process them.
- Price a part in every material, cheapest first, or in one material with price breaks and delivery dates.
- Build saved carts of cut-only parts and return a checkout URL the account owner opens to pay. Nothing is ordered here.
- List and delete parts and saved carts, and list materials with their services.
